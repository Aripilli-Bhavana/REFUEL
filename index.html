Frequently Asked Questions

What is Refuel?

Refuel is an on-demand fuel delivery service that brings fuel directly to your vehicle step, eliminating the need to visit the gas station visits.

How does Refuel ensure fuel quality?

We source fuel directly from authorized suppliers like Indian Oil, Hindustan Petroleum and Bharat Petroleum to ensure it meets the highest quality and safety standards.

How do I place an order?

Simply visit our website, go to the Order Here section, select your fuel type, quantity, location, and confirm payment.

Can I schedule a fuel delivery in advance?

Yes! You can place a Preorder and select your preferred delivery date and time.

How long does delivery take?

We deliver fuel directly from our partnered fuel stations nearest to your location. Our standard delivery time depends on your location and demand, but we aim to get your fuel to you as quickly as possible.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payments via credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets.

Is doorstep fuel delivery legal?

Yes, we comply with all government regulations and safety protocols for fuel transportation and delivery.

What safety measures does Refuel follow?

Our vehicles are equipped with certified fuel storage tanks, fire safety equipment, and trained personnel to handle fuel transportation securely.

Can I order fuel for my car at home?

Absolutely! We deliver fuel for personal vehicles, fleets, and businesses.
